TimeLens

Hover a timestamp on any web page and a tooltip shows it in your own time zone, with your local date and how long ago or how far away it is.

Formats it reads

  • ISO 8601, such as 2026-11-09T14:47:00Z
  • Database and log times, such as 2026-11-09 14:47:00.226
  • Unix time in seconds or milliseconds
  • Email and web dates, such as Mon, 09 Nov 2026 20:17:00 +0530
  • Written dates, such as Nov 9 14:47 UTC
  • Times with a zone or offset, such as 16:00 GMT+5:30

Private by design

TimeLens reads only the text under your pointer, on your device. It saves only your settings, in your browser. It makes no network requests. Read the full privacy policy.
