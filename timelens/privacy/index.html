TimeLens privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-12

TimeLens is published by Cosmos. It shows times found on web pages in your local time zone.

What TimeLens does with data

  • Page content: when you move the pointer over a page, TimeLens reads the text under the pointer, on your device only, to find a timestamp and show it in a tooltip. Page text is never stored, logged or transmitted.
  • Settings: your preferences (on or off, and the theme) are saved with the browser storage API. They stay in your browser's local storage and are never sent anywhere.
  • No collection: TimeLens does not collect, sell or share personal data, browsing history or analytics.
  • No network requests: TimeLens makes no network requests and loads no remote code.
  • No third parties: no advertising, tracking or analytics services are used.

Limited Use

The use of information received by TimeLens adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Page text is used only to show the tooltip you see, and is not transferred, sold, or used for any other purpose.
