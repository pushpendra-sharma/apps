Common questions

Nothing happens when I hover a time

  • Check that TimeLens is switched on. Click the toolbar icon and look at the Enabled switch.
  • Reload the page. Pages that were open before you installed TimeLens need a reload.
  • Chrome doesn't let extensions run on some pages, such as chrome:// pages and the Chrome Web Store.

Why is a zone like PST or IST left alone?

Many abbreviations mean more than one zone. IST can be India, Ireland or Israel, for example. Write the offset instead, such as GMT+5:30 or UTC-8, and TimeLens converts it.

The tooltip says a date or zone was assumed

When a time has no zone, TimeLens reads it as UTC. When it has no date, it uses today. The note in the tooltip tells you which one was assumed.

How do I change the theme or turn TimeLens off?

Click the TimeLens icon in the Chrome toolbar. Use Enabled to turn it on or off, and Theme to choose light, dark or system.

Does TimeLens collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy.
